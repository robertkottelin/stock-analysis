◆ The most predictive driver — three converging lenses

The master driver is the growth of client AUM and the fee result — the capital-light flywheel that converts to organic capital generation and the dividend. Reported IFRS earnings are noise; as a dividend instrument, the price is also swung by capital-return signals and share flow.

Lens 1 — what moves the price (empirical)
Fee/AUM growth & flows
The stock rises on the fee-and-AUM story and shrugs off IFRS noise. Q3'25: fee result +20%, record AUM → shares +5%. Q1'26: a one-off IFRS technical loss from a discount-rate change, yet capital-light profit +35% and solvency hit 203% — the loss was ignored. The single biggest move was non-fundamental: Altor's €238m block sale, −8%.
Lens 2 — what moves fair value (computed)
The fee multiple, then capital
On a sum-of-the-parts (Module A), the biggest fair-value lever is the multiple the market awards the fee business — i.e. its embedded growth expectation. How much excess capital is ultimately returned is a close second. The volatile net finance result barely moves value even as it dominates reported EPS.
Lens 3 — what management & analysts track
OCG > EPS; the DDM
Management states organic capital generation is "a better indication of dividend capacity than EPS." Targets are set on capital-light profit (>10%/yr), ROE (>20%) and a >€1bn cumulative payout. Inderes and peers value the stock on a dividend-discount model, not earnings.
Synthesis. The most predictive driver is client-AUM / fee-result growth — it is upstream of everything: AUM → fee result → capital-light profit → organic capital generation → the dividend the DDM (and the ~15% yield) capitalizes. Reported earnings, swung by discount-rate mark-to-market, are a distraction the market has learned to ignore. Two caveats specific to this stock: because it trades right on analysts' DDM fair value as a high-yield vehicle, the price is unusually sensitive to capital-return execution (deploying the post-Saxo excess solvency) and to share overhang (the Altor exit). Watch net flows, AUM and the fee result — not the headline profit line.

Net flows turn negative. Client AUM net flow prints negative for two consecutive quarters — the flywheel that feeds fees and capital generation has stalled.

MC engine (Module A, re-used)Fair value per share = engine(driver draws) drawn from correlated triangulars with a one-factor copula and a regime-shock mixture. Exact formula per name is inside the pipeline's Module A footer.
Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.
Forward P/E (Module I)Live spot ÷ the sourced consensus EPS estimate for the current fiscal year (≈ next 12 months) and the next fiscal year (≈ 24 months). Trailing P/E uses the reported TTM EPS. A non-positive EPS (loss year) renders as n/m.
Payoff-asymmetry ratio (Module I)(P(FV>price) × E[FV−price | FV>price]) ÷ (P(FV<price) × E[price−FV | FV<price]). Values > 1× = pay-off skew in your favour.
Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.
Kelly f* (Module I)(b·p − q)/b, where p = P(FV>price), q = 1−p. Fraction of bank-roll to size if the MC is truth. Half-Kelly (or lower) is the institutional convention.
VaR & CVaR (Module I)VaRα = the α-quantile of (FV/price − 1) — the loss threshold. CVaRα = mean of the α% worst paths — Expected Shortfall.
Realised volatility (Module I)SD of daily log-returns × √252, over trailing 1y (252 sessions) and 3y (756 sessions).
Beta (Module I)OLS slope of daily stock log-returns on benchmark log-returns over trailing 252 sessions. R² reported alongside.
Max drawdown (Module I)mint(Pt/max(Ps≤t) − 1) over 5-year adjusted closes.
Momentum 1/3/6/12m (Module I)Simple return over trailing 21 / 63 / 126 / 252 sessions on adjusted close.
Own-history percentile (Module I)Fraction of trailing 5-year daily closes ≤ current spot.
Peer 12m relative return (Module I)Stock's 12m return minus peer's 12m return, both simple returns on adjusted close.

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Fair value per share = capital-light PBT × (1 − 22% tax) × the fee multiple, plus distributable excess capital, ÷ 503m shares. Each driver is drawn from a triangular distribution: capital-light PBT Tri(78, 92, 118 €m), fee multiple (bounds 17–34×, your mode), distributable capital (bounds €0.55–1.5bn, your mode). Dependence: a one-factor copula (loadings — fee multiple 0.75, capital-light PBT 0.65, distributable capital 0.35, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob sets the drawdown probability). Correlation 0 and tail 0 reproduce the independent model. Tornado, scenario anchors and the Bayesian posterior are all computed live.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: fetched from a citable source (Mandatum releases, earnings calls, Simply Wall St / S&P, MarketScreener, Inderes). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation, the SOTP, capital-quality ratios, Mandatum's own multiples). Your assumption: an input you set (fee multiple, distributable capital, correlation, tail, scenario priors, evidence weights, kill thresholds).
