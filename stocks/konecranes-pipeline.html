◆ The most predictive driver — three converging lenses

There is no single commodity-style driver. The master variable is the durability of the record ~14% EBITA margin — but the most predictive mover of the share price right now is volume (order → sales conversion).

Lens 1 — what moves the price (empirical)
Orders, volume & guidance
The stock reacts to the demand cycle, not the margin. Feb 2026: record 14% margin and +12% orders, yet the stock fell ~10–14% on a soft order quarter + cautious guidance. Apr 2026: record Q1 margin, yet −7% on a ~5% sales decline.
Lens 2 — what moves fair value (computed)
The multiple, then the margin
The tornado (Module B) shows the EV/EBITA multiple is the single biggest lever on intrinsic value, with the EBITA margin close behind — together dwarfing revenue and net cash. The multiple is itself the market's verdict on margin durability.
Lens 3 — what the largest owner says
Service growth + equipment margin
Solidium (11% holder, Finnish State) names Konecranes' value drivers as "continuous growth of the maintenance business" and "permanently raising the profitability of the new-equipment business."
Synthesis. The three reconcile: volume is the leading domino. The ~14% margin is already a self-help record (management says the "low-hanging fruit" is gone), so what decides whether it holds is operating leverage from volume — and whether the market awards a quality multiple depends on the same demand signal. So watch order intake and book-to-bill, not the margin headline. The constructive tell as of Q1'26: the order book is at a 3-year-high €3.2bn and book-to-bill is 1.2 — the leading indicator is green even as reported sales lag.

Rate-limited but reliable for cash equities and major indices; used as the single source of truth for all live-fetched price series in Modules I & J.
  • Primary company releases — direct company disclosures (Finnish issuers publish concurrently in Finnish and English, ESMA-compliant). Every balance-sheet, income-statement and cash-flow figure inside Modules A / D / E traces to a release listed above.
  • Solidium / Finnish State ownership — the State's holding company and Prime Minister's Office publish the strategic-interest register directly.
  • Simply Wall St (S&P Global Market Intelligence) — used to cross-check balance-sheet ratios (coverage, gearing) where the primary release aggregates line items.
  • Analyst consensus — pulled from Investing.com and MarketScreener at time of write; consensus PTs are point-in-time and can move.

◆ Every metric computed here — formulas

MetricFormula / definition
MC engine (Module A, re-used)Fair value per share = engine(driver draws) drawn from correlated triangulars with a one-factor copula and a regime-shock mixture. Exact formula per name is inside the pipeline's Module A footer.
Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.
Payoff-asymmetry ratio (Module I)(P(FV>price) × E[FV−price | FV>price]) ÷ (P(FV<price) × E[price−FV | FV<price]). Values > 1× = pay-off skew in your favour.
Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.
Kelly f* (Module I)(b·p − q)/b, where p = P(FV>price), q = 1−p. Fraction of bank-roll to size if the MC is truth. Half-Kelly (or lower) is the institutional convention.
VaR & CVaR (Module I)VaRα = the α-quantile of (FV/price − 1) — the loss threshold. CVaRα = mean of the α% worst paths — Expected Shortfall.
Realised volatility (Module I)SD of daily log-returns × √252, over trailing 1y (252 sessions) and 3y (756 sessions).
Beta (Module I)OLS slope of daily stock log-returns on benchmark log-returns over trailing 252 sessions. R² reported alongside.
Max drawdown (Module I)mint(Pt/max(Ps≤t) − 1) over 5-year adjusted closes.
Momentum 1/3/6/12m (Module I)Simple return over trailing 21 / 63 / 126 / 252 sessions on adjusted close.
Own-history percentile (Module I)Fraction of trailing 5-year daily closes ≤ current spot.
Peer 12m relative return (Module I)Stock's 12m return minus peer's 12m return, both simple returns on adjusted close.
Return base rates (Module J)Empirical frequencies over the 5-year rolling windows: P(63-day return < −10%), P(252-day return < 0), P(252-day return > +15%).
Factor composite V/Q/M/L (Module J)V = 1 − price-percentile in own 5y history; Q = quality anchor from Module D (Piotroski / Altman / ROCE); M = logistic-scaled (12m − 1m) return; L = 1 − (1y realised vol / 45%). Simple mean × 100.
Owner-earnings yield − real rate (Module J)OE yield = pipeline Module D FCF / market cap; real rate = ^TNX (fetched at build) minus a 2.0% implied inflation. Spread in bp.

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Each driver is drawn from a triangular distribution: EBITA margin (bounds 10.5–16.0%, your mode), EV/EBITA multiple (bounds 7.5–14.5×, your mode), sales Tri(3.95, 4.25, 4.65 bn), net cash Tri(50, 185, 400 m). Fair value = multiple × (sales × margin) + net cash, ÷ 237.64m shares. Dependence: a one-factor copula (loadings — margin 0.75, sales 0.70, multiple 0.65, net cash 0.20, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob sets the downturn probability). Correlation 0 and tail 0 reproduce the independent model. Tornado, scenario anchors and the Bayesian posterior are all computed live.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: fetched from a citable source (Konecranes releases, S&P/Simply Wall St, Solidium, Investing.com, GuruFocus). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation, forensic scores, Konecranes' own multiples). Your assumption: an input you set (mid-cycle margin, central multiple, correlation, tail, scenario priors, evidence weights, kill thresholds).

Still excluded — post-Modules-I&J audit

Still legitimately excluded — this build kept these out, not faked: Beneish M-score — needs eight two-year ratios not fully disclosed; a half-estimated score is worse than none. Options skew, precise borrow cost, and detailed insider transaction flow — not reliably fetchable for a Nasdaq Helsinki name from a public API; would slot in from a Bloomberg terminal. An exact forward multiple — needs a consensus forward-EBITDA figure not asserted as fact. Everything else previously flagged as "cut" (factor betas, positioning realised-vol, own-history percentile, 12-month peer relative return, base-rate frequencies, owner-earnings yield vs real rate) is now included in Modules I & J and computed live from the Yahoo Finance v8 chart API.
