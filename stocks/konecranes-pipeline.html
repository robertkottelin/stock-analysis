Business thesis & macro place

What Konecranes is, where the cash comes from, and how it sits in robotics · AI · data · energy · logistics trends.
Sourced + judgment
MIXED · LEANS BULLA high-quality industrial compounder with a service annuity and a port/equipment cycle on top — not an AI pure-play, but a real infrastructure supplier to automation, global goods flow, and the energy build-out. The fundamentals (Modules Q · D · E · G · F) support quality; the tape is still pricing the cycle, not the secular stack.

What the company is. Konecranes Plc (HEL: KCR) designs, manufactures and services lifting equipment for factories, process industries and ports. Two cash engines dominate the economics: Industrial Equipment + Service (process cranes, standard cranes, a large installed-base maintenance franchise at ~22% margin) and Port Solutions (ship-to-shore cranes, yard cranes, straddle carriers, software and automation for container terminals). FY2025 sales ≈ €4.25bn, comparable EBITA margin a record ~14%, net cash €185m, ROCE ~21%.

Why the share price still feels “cyclical.” Equipment orders and port projects swing with global industrial production and container throughput. Service is stickier and higher-margin, but the market still marks the whole name to the order book and guidance tone (see Module B). That is the tension this report is built around: quality books + secular automation exposure, trading like a mid-cycle capital-goods name.

◆ Place in the macro stack — five trend lenses

Konecranes does not sell foundation models or GPUs. It sells the physical layer that moves steel, containers, turbines and factory components. The relevant question is which macro waves actually touch that layer — and with what intensity.

1 · Robotics & automation
Port + yard automation
Automated container terminals, remote-operated STS/RTG cranes, AGVs and software are the clearest robotics exposure. Capex cycles at big ports are lumpy, but once automated, the installed base deepens the service annuity. This is Konecranes’ most direct “robotics” call option — not humanoids, industrial automation.
2 · AI (real, not narrative)
Predictive service & ops software
AI here means condition monitoring, predictive maintenance on the installed crane base, and terminal operating optimisation — software that raises service attach and uptime, not a hyperscaler revenue line. Material for margin durability in Service; not a second valuation multiple by itself.
3 · Computing & data centres
Indirect construction demand
Hyperscale and grid build-outs need heavy lift, industrial construction and process equipment. Konecranes is a secondary beneficiary (cranes on site, process industry customers), not a pure-play data-centre OEM. Treat as a mild tailwind to Industrial, not the thesis core.
4 · Data & global logistics
Container trade & e-commerce flow
Port Solutions lives on container throughput, fleet replacement and terminal productivity. Digitised logistics and e-commerce raise the bar on terminal efficiency — supporting automation spend — while trade volumes set the cycle. Book-to-bill and order book are still the near-term gauges.
5 · Energy transition
Wind, power & process industry
Industrial cranes and lifting solutions serve wind (components/install support), power generation, and heavy process industries tied to the energy complex. A multi-year infrastructure cycle is a constructive backdrop for Industrial Equipment, again via orders and service, not commodity energy prices directly.
Investment thesis (one paragraph). Own Konecranes if you want a cash-generative automation & lifting franchise with a fortress balance sheet, a growing service book, and optionality on port automation and industrial infrastructure — at a multiple still closer to “machinery median” than to quality compounders (Module G). Fade it if you need pure-play AI/software exposure, or if you believe the industrial/port cycle is rolling over hard enough to unwind the ~14% margin (Modules A–C). The constructive near-term tell remains order book €3.2bn · book-to-bill 1.2 (Q1’26) against a de-rated tape.

Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.

Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Each driver is drawn from a triangular distribution: EBITA margin (bounds 10.5–16.0%, your mode), EV/EBITA multiple (bounds 7.5–14.5×, your mode), sales Tri(3.95, 4.25, 4.65 bn), net cash Tri(50, 185, 400 m). Fair value = multiple × (sales × margin) + net cash, ÷ 237.64m shares. Dependence: a one-factor copula (loadings — margin 0.75, sales 0.70, multiple 0.65, net cash 0.20, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob sets the downturn probability). Correlation 0 and tail 0 reproduce the independent model. Tornado, scenario anchors and the Bayesian posterior are all computed live.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: fetched from a citable source (Konecranes releases, S&P/Simply Wall St, Solidium, Investing.com, GuruFocus). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation, forensic scores, Konecranes' own multiples). Your assumption: an input you set (mid-cycle margin, central multiple, correlation, tail, scenario priors, evidence weights, kill thresholds).
