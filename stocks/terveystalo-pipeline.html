◆ The most predictive driver — three converging lenses

There is no commodity spread here. The defensive ballast is the Healthcare Services clinic network, but the most predictive mover of the share price is occupational-health & clinic demand — and, right behind it, the EV/EBIT multiple the market grants that core.

Lens 1 — what moves the price (empirical)
The demand cycle
Terveystalo compounded its adjusted-EBITA margin from 8.4% (2022) to a record 14.0% (2025) on an efficiency programme — yet the stock de-rated, because volume rolled over: Q1'26 revenue fell −11% and FY2026 adjusted-EBIT guidance was cut to €135–165m (2025: €156m). The tape tracks occupational-health and private-pay volume, not the margin headline.
Lens 2 — what moves fair value (computed)
HS profit, then the core multiple
The tornado (Module B) ranks Healthcare Services adjusted EBIT as the single biggest lever on intrinsic value, with the core EV/EBIT multiple just behind. The Portfolio Businesses (incl. Sweden) and its EV/Sales multiple are second-order.
Lens 3 — what management & the anchor back
Management leads on adjusted EBIT and is buying Silmäasema (eye care) at 11.5× EV/EBIT — €275m cash + 36.5m new shares — making Coronaria the ~15.1% top holder. The stated value drivers are efficiency, digital/occupational-health share, and eye-care scale.
Synthesis. The three reconcile on one tension: the market prices Terveystalo's core at roughly 8.5× EV/EBIT (reverse-DCF, Module I) — below the 11.5× the company itself just agreed to pay for Silmäasema and below Nordic-quality health-services comps. Either the core is cheap, or the market disbelieves the record margin will survive the demand downturn. A ~7.8% dividend and a ~16% owner-earnings yield pay you to wait; the swing — and the re-rating — lives in whether Healthcare Services volume stabilises. The payoff is roughly symmetric at the base case (~52% of paths above price) with a genuinely fat left tail if demand keeps sliding.

MetricFormula / definition
MC engine (Module A, re-used)Fair value per share = engine(driver draws) drawn from correlated triangulars with a one-factor copula and a regime-shock mixture. Exact formula per name is inside the pipeline's Module A footer.
Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.
Payoff-asymmetry ratio (Module I)(P(FV>price) × E[FV−price | FV>price]) ÷ (P(FV<price) × E[price−FV | FV<price]). Values > 1× = pay-off skew in your favour.
Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.
Kelly f* (Module I)(b·p − q)/b, where p = P(FV>price), q = 1−p. Fraction of bank-roll to size if the MC is truth. Half-Kelly (or lower) is the institutional convention.
VaR & CVaR (Module I)VaRα = the α-quantile of (FV/price − 1) — the loss threshold. CVaRα = mean of the α% worst paths — Expected Shortfall.
Realised volatility (Module I)SD of daily log-returns × √252, over trailing 1y (252 sessions) and 3y (756 sessions).

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Each driver is drawn from a triangular distribution: Healthcare Services adjusted EBIT (bounds 116–158 €m, your mode), core EV/EBIT multiple (bounds 6.3–11.0×, your mode), Portfolio Businesses revenue Tri(210, 248, 282 €m), Portfolio EV/Sales Tri(0.32, 0.70, 1.08×). Fair value = HS + Portfolio − ~€30m capitalised central cost − €305.4m net debt (ex-lease), ÷ 126.655m shares. Dependence: a one-factor copula (loadings — HS EBIT 0.66, core multiple 0.64, Portfolio revenue 0.48, Portfolio multiple 0.43, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob sets the demand-downturn probability). Correlation 0 and tail 0 reproduce the independent model. Tornado, scenario anchors and the Bayesian posterior are all computed live. The Silmäasema acquisition is deliberately excluded from the base engine.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: fetched from a citable source (Terveystalo releases, Silmäasema deal release, ownership register, stockanalysis.com/Simply Wall St, Inderes/MarketScreener). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation, forensic scores, reverse-DCF, Kelly/CVaR). Your assumption: an input you set (HS adjusted EBIT, core multiple, correlation, tail, scenario priors, evidence weights, kill thresholds).

Still excluded — post-Modules-I&J audit

Still legitimately excluded — this build kept these out, not faked: The two-leg EV/EBIT sum-of-the-parts is a transparent proxy, not a hidden number. Terveystalo discloses segment adjusted operating profit but not a segment balance sheet, so Healthcare Services is valued on EV/EBIT, Portfolio Businesses (near break-even at EBIT) on EV/Sales, and IFRS-16 lease liabilities (~€203m) are excluded from net debt — matched by right-of-use assets, with the clinic rent already inside segment EBIT via right-of-use depreciation. Valuing on lease-inclusive EV/EBITDA instead would move the mix, not the conclusion. The Silmaasema acquisition is modelled as an explicit driver/scenario, not baked into the base engine — the 36.5m new shares (~+29% count) and €275m cash consideration change the equity story materially and the deal was still completing, so the base case values Terveystalo standalone (126.655m shares) and Modules B/C/H carry the pro-forma math openly. Beneish M-score — needs eight two-year ratios not fully disclosed for an acquisition-built P&L; a half-estimated score is worse than none. Options skew, precise borrow cost, and detailed insider transaction flow — not reliably fetchable for a Nasdaq Helsinki name from a public API; would slot in from a Bloomberg terminal. Everything else previously flagged as "cut" (factor betas, own-history percentile, momentum, realised vol, drawdown, 12-month peer relative return, base-rate frequencies, owner-earnings yield vs real rate) is included in Modules I & J and computed live from the Yahoo Finance v8 chart API.
