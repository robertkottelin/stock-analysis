How to read this module. Use the Kelly f* and payoff asymmetry as a two-column decision matrix: both positive = the MC is telling you to lean in (size below Kelly, always); both negative = the MC is telling you the market has already reached — or over-shot — your base case. A wonderful business at a full-to-slightly-rich price. The one clear bull is forensic quality (D): cash generation, margins, and the balance sheet are underwritable today. Central fair value (~$224) sits below the $247 tape, so Modules A/C lean cautious: the market is already paying for AWS mid-30s growth to hold and for a ~32× EV/EBIT on that block. The tornado (B) is unambiguous — the multiple on AWS earnings matters as much as the earnings themselves. Posture: own the quality if you already do; add on weakness toward ~$220–230 (mode region) or on evidence the reverse-DCF multiple compresses without growth breaking; do not chase into 52-week highs on narrative alone.

◆ Data providers & the audit rule

  • Yahoo Finance v8 chart API — public, non-authenticated JSON endpoint. Returns adjusted close, OHLC, volume and meta (regular-market-price, 52w range). Rate-limited but reliable for cash equities and major indices; used as the single source of truth for all live-fetched price series in Modules I & J.
  • Primary company releases — direct company disclosures (Finnish issuers publish concurrently in Finnish and English, ESMA-compliant). Every balance-sheet, income-statement and cash-flow figure inside Modules A / D / E traces to a release listed above.
  • Solidium / Finnish State ownership — the State's holding company and Prime Minister's Office publish the strategic-interest register directly.
  • Simply Wall St (S&P Global Market Intelligence) — used to cross-check balance-sheet ratios (coverage, gearing) where the primary release aggregates line items.
  • Analyst consensus — pulled from Investing.com and MarketScreener at time of write; consensus PTs are point-in-time and can move.

◆ Every metric computed here — formulas

MetricFormula / definition
MC engine (Module A, re-used)Fair value per share = engine(driver draws) drawn from correlated triangulars with a one-factor copula and a regime-shock mixture. Exact formula per name is inside the pipeline's Module A footer.
Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.
Forward P/E (Module I)Live spot ÷ the sourced consensus EPS estimate for the current fiscal year (≈ next 12 months) and the next fiscal year (≈ 24 months). Trailing P/E uses the reported TTM EPS. A non-positive EPS (loss year) renders as n/m.
Payoff-asymmetry ratio (Module I)(P(FV>price) × E[FV−price | FV>price]) ÷ (P(FV<price) × E[price−FV | FV<price]). Values > 1× = pay-off skew in your favour.
Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.
Kelly f* (Module I)(b·p − q)/b, where p = P(FV>price), q = 1−p. Fraction of bank-roll to size if the MC is truth. Half-Kelly (or lower) is the institutional convention.
VaR & CVaR (Module I)VaRα = the α-quantile of (FV/price − 1) — the loss threshold. CVaRα = mean of the α% worst paths — Expected Shortfall.
Realised volatility (Module I)SD of daily log-returns × √252, over trailing 1y (252 sessions) and 3y (756 sessions).
Beta (Module I)OLS slope of daily stock log-returns on benchmark log-returns over trailing 252 sessions. R² reported alongside.
Max drawdown (Module I)mint(Pt/max(Ps≤t) − 1) over 5-year adjusted closes.
Momentum 1/3/6/12m (Module I)Simple return over trailing 21 / 63 / 126 / 252 sessions on adjusted close.
Own-history percentile (Module I)Fraction of trailing 5-year daily closes ≤ current spot.
Peer 12m relative return (Module I)Stock's 12m return minus peer's 12m return, both simple returns on adjusted close.
Return base rates (Module J)Empirical frequencies over the 5-year rolling windows: P(63-day return < −10%), P(252-day return < 0), P(252-day return > +15%).
Factor composite V/Q/M/L (Module J)V = 1 − price-percentile in own 5y history; Q = quality anchor from Module D (Piotroski / Altman / ROCE); M = logistic-scaled (12m − 1m) return; L = 1 − (1y realised vol / 45%). Simple mean × 100.
Owner-earnings yield − real rate (Module J)OE yield = pipeline Module D FCF / market cap; real rate = ^TNX (fetched at build) minus a 2.0% implied inflation. Spread in bp.

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Fair value per share = (AWS revenue × margin% × AWS EV/EBIT multiple + non-AWS operating income × earnings multiple + net cash $12bn) × 1,000 ÷ 10,837m shares. Five triangular marginals: AWS revenue $150–172bn (default mid $161bn); AWS margin 30–39% (mid 35%); AWS multiple 18–38× (mid 28×); non-AWS OI $34–50bn (mid $42bn); non-AWS multiple 14–26× (mid 20×). One-factor copula loadings: awsrev 0.75, awsmgn 0.55, awsmlt 0.70, retoi 0.65, retmlt 0.55, scaled by the correlation knob; regime-stress mixture on the tail knob. Advertising is inside the non-AWS block because Amazon does not disclose ads operating income.

The three labels

Sourced: 8-K / 10-K / Yahoo fundamentals / consensus trackers. Computed: engine outputs, ratios, reverse-DCF, Kelly. Your assumption: mid-points, correlation, tail, scenario priors, kill thresholds.

Still excluded

Still legitimately excluded — this build kept these out, not faked: Beneish M-score — needs eight two-year ratios at a granularity Amazon does not break out per segment; a half-estimated score is worse than none. Advertising segment operating income — embedded in the North America / International segments and never disclosed, which is why the engine values a single non-AWS block instead of ads separately — a modelling simplification, not a hidden number. Options skew, stock-borrow cost, and daily insider-transaction net flow — fetchable for a US mega-cap from paid terminals but not from the Yahoo v8 chart endpoint this toolkit standardises on; they would slot in without framework changes. A clean ex-AI-gains TTM EPS — the $16.8bn Q1'26 Anthropic mark-up sits inside reported EPS; stripping it requires a tax-rate assumption this build does not assert. Everything else previously flagged as "cut" (factor betas vs S&P 500 / Nasdaq 100 / rates, price-history percentile, momentum, realised vol, drawdown, peer 12m relative, base-rate frequencies, owner-earnings yield vs real rate) is included in Modules I & J and computed live from the Yahoo Finance v8 chart API.
