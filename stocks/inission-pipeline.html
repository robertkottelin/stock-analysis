◆ The most predictive driver — three converging lenses

There is no commodity spread here. The industrial ballast is the EMS contract-manufacturing base (medtech, defence, data centres, electrification), but the most predictive mover of the share price is order intake and EBITA margin — and, right behind it, the EV/EBITA multiple the market grants a mid-cap Nordic EMS.

Lens 1 — what moves the price (empirical)
Order book → margin expansion
The stock roughly doubled from the SEK 36 52-week low as H2'25 / Q1'26 converted a high book-to-bill into both volume and margin: Q1 sales +37% (organic +26%), EBITA margin 7.0% vs 4.1%. The tape tracks industrial demand (defence, data centres, quantum/industrial electronics) more than the balance sheet.
Lens 2 — what moves fair value (computed)
Sales × margin, then the multiple
The tornado (Module B) ranks EBITA margin and sales as the two biggest levers on intrinsic value, with the EV/EBITA multiple close behind. Net debt is second-order once FCF is positive.
Lens 3 — what management & the anchor back
Management targets FY2026 sales SEK 2.3–2.5bn and EBITA margin >6%, then medium-term growth >15% (10% organic + 5% M&A) and EBITA margin >9%. ABG (commissioned) marks adj. EBITA ~6.8% for 2026e and a SEK 50–90 fair-value range. Insider ownership remains high (~52%).

Correlated MC median (~kr59) sits below the mode point-estimate (~kr67) and below spot (~kr68) — a genuine two-sided setup that currently tilts against adding risk at the print.

Thesis. Inission is a Nordic EMS roll-up with a real operational inflection: Q1'26 sales +37% (organic +26%), EBITA margin 7.0%, Power OEM back in the black, backlog SEK 1.5bn. Perfect-execution Module U prices Y10 FV ~kr321 (16.7% CAGR) if that path is delivered.

What the tools say (computed live). Reverse-DCF implies only 11.7× EV/EBITA at mode sales/margin — essentially the pipeline's own 11.5× mode, so the market is not pricing a heroic multiple. The problem is the distribution: correlated MC median kr58.6 vs live spot kr68.4, P(FV>price) only 31%, payoff asymmetry 0.31× (expected loss dominates), Kelly −69%, and the share sits at the 96th percentile of its own 5-year history. Module I is therefore BEAR on the price; Modules B/E/G/U remain BULL on the business and long-term ceiling.

Why not a clean BULL or BEAR? The business just printed its best quarter in years and cash conversion is real — fighting the fundamental tape is unwise. But after a double from SEK 36, LTM EV/EBITA ~14.8× is no longer deep value, inventory is heavy, float is thin, and the >9% margin target sits above company history (peak 7.4% in 2023). This is a quality-of-execution story at a full price — not a free option on mean reversion, and not a short of a broken company.

Conviction. Medium-low on adding risk here; medium on the operational trajectory. Size for liquidity and the industrial cycle. The single variable that resolves the setup is EBITA-margin durability through H2'26 and into 2027; watch book-to-bill and Power OEM LTM margin, not the daily tape.

◆ Every metric computed here — formulas

MetricFormula / definition
MC engine (Module A, re-used)Fair value per share = engine(driver draws) drawn from correlated triangulars with a one-factor copula and a regime-shock mixture. Exact formula per name is inside the pipeline's Module A footer.
Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.
Forward P/E (Module I)Live spot ÷ the sourced consensus EPS estimate for the current fiscal year (≈ next 12 months) and the next fiscal year (≈ 24 months). Trailing P/E uses the reported TTM EPS. A non-positive EPS (loss year) renders as n/m.
Payoff-asymmetry ratio (Module I)(P(FV>price) × E[FV−price | FV>price]) ÷ (P(FV<price) × E[price−FV | FV<price]). Values > 1× = pay-off skew in your favour.
Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.
Kelly f* (Module I)(b·p − q)/b, where p = P(FV>price), q = 1−p. Fraction of bank-roll to size if the MC is truth. Half-Kelly (or lower) is the institutional convention.
VaR & CVaR (Module I)VaRα = the α-quantile of (FV/price − 1) — the loss threshold. CVaRα = mean of the α% worst paths — Expected Shortfall.
Realised volatility (Module I)SD of daily log-returns × √252, over trailing 1y (252 sessions) and 3y (756 sessions).
Beta (Module I)OLS slope of daily stock log-returns on benchmark log-returns over trailing 252 sessions. R² reported alongside.
Max drawdown (Module I)mint(Pt/max(Ps≤t) − 1) over 5-year adjusted closes.
Momentum 1/3/6/12m (Module I)Simple return over trailing 21 / 63 / 126 / 252 sessions on adjusted close.
Own-history percentile (Module I)Fraction of trailing 5-year daily closes ≤ current spot.
Peer 12m relative return (Module I)Stock's 12m return minus peer's 12m return, both simple returns on adjusted close.
Return base rates (Module J)Empirical frequencies over the 5-year rolling windows: P(63-day return < −10%), P(252-day return < 0), P(252-day return > +15%).
Factor composite V/Q/M/L (Module J)V = 1 − price-percentile in own 5y history; Q = quality anchor from Module D (Piotroski / Altman / ROCE); M = logistic-scaled (12m − 1m) return; L = 1 − (1y realised vol / 45%). Simple mean × 100.
Owner-earnings yield − real rate (Module J)OE yield = pipeline Module D FCF / market cap; real rate = ^TNX (fetched at build) minus a 2.0% implied inflation. Spread in bp.

Engine footnote

20,000 paths. Each driver is drawn from a triangular distribution: sales Tri(2100, 2550, 2900 MSEK), EBITA margin Tri(4.0, 6.8, 8.5%), EV/EBITA Tri(7.5, 11.5, 14.5×), net debt Tri(280, 450, 620 MSEK). Fair value = (sales × mgn/100 × mlt − nd) ÷ 23.05m shares. Dependence: one-factor copula (loadings — sales 0.70, margin 0.72, multiple 0.60, net debt −0.25) plus a regime-shock mixture. Correlation 0 and tail 0 reproduce the independent model.

Still legitimately excluded

Still legitimately excluded — this build kept these out, not faked: The single-leg EBITA × EV/EBITA engine is a transparent proxy, not a hidden number. Inission reports two segments (EMS + Power OEM) but the market prices the group on consolidated EBITA; segment-level SOTP would mainly re-allocate the same profit.
