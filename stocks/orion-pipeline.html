Why this matters. Orion's distribution is far tighter than a cyclical refiner's — the diversified base (generics, consumer health, animal health, Fermion) puts a floor under fair value that a single-commodity business doesn't have. The tail risk here is a re-rating risk, not an operating-collapse risk: correlation barely moves the median but roughly doubles the chance of a deep-loss outcome (below €54, ≈a 23% drawdown from price) at the default settings. Because the Nubeqa®-run-rate driver and its multiple share a high loading on the common factor (see Module B), they don't just add in the tails — they compound.

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Fair value = (Nubeqa®-complex run-rate × its multiple + rest-of-Orion net sales × its multiple + pipeline optionality − net debt €131.0m, Q1'26) ÷ 140.69m shares. Five triangular marginals: Nubeqa®-booked run-rate €700–1,050m around your mid (default €900m); its EV/sales multiple 5.5–9.0×; rest-of-Orion (generics, consumer health, animal health, Fermion) net sales €1,030–1,200m; its multiple 1.8–3.5×; pipeline optionality €0–500m. Dependence: a one-factor copula (each driver loads on a common factor — Nubeqa® run-rate 0.75, its multiple 0.55, rest-of-Orion sales 0.25, its multiple 0.20, pipeline 0.15, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob sets the stress probability). Correlation = 0 and tail = 0 reproduce the independent model exactly. Both halves are valued on EV/sales rather than EV/EBIT because Orion discloses only one IFRS 8 segment — divisional EBIT isn't available, so this is a modelling simplification, not a hidden number. Tornado, scenario anchors and the Bayesian posterior are all computed live. The loadings reflect relative cyclicality — a modelling choice, not estimated coefficients.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: a figure fetched from a citable source (Orion's own releases, Investing.com, MarketScreener, Fiscal.ai, patent trackers, Orion's investor-relations pages). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation outputs, the forensic scores, Orion's own EV/EBITDA, the payout ratios). Your assumption: an input you set that is judgment, not data (Nubeqa® run-rate mid, correlation, tail probability, scenario priors, evidence likelihood-ratios, kill thresholds).
