Margin nowcast — the decomposition model

Realised margin from public price legs, with a one-quarter lag and Monte Carlo bands.
Computed · new model 2026-07-02
MIXEDConstructive near term, honest about fragility. Elevated H2 with a possible Q3 peak, fading into Q4; the windfall-stripped mid-cycle strip lands at $575–683/t — bracketing the pipeline’s $630 base case and validating the uptrend as structural.

The equation. European spot-margin proxy, per tonne of product:

Spot proxy [$/t] = (0.845 × Gasoil [$/t] + HVO Class II premium [$/m³]) ÷ 0.78  −  1.2 × UCO [$/t]  −  $300 conversion
Realisedt  ≈  capture (≈0.87) × Spot proxyt−1   // the term book lags spot by ~1 quarter

0.845 converts gasoil $/t to $/m³ (density); 0.78 t/m³ converts HVO back to tonnes; 1.2 t feedstock per t product (~83% yield, byproduct credits ≈ offset); $300/t conversion+logistics is an assumption. The capture × lag structure is the economics of a ~2/3 diesel-indexed term book: today’s spot is next quarter’s realised price.

Where this model breaks. Calibration rests on two clean points (n=2, not a regression). If Neste’s term book resets annually (2026 premiums largely negotiated late 2025) rather than rolling quarterly, the Q3 hump mutes and margins flatten near the negotiated level — still elevated, different shape. The US business, SAF premia and credits sit outside the EU-only proxy (they currently push capture up — D4 near record). Feedstock is the ceiling: UCO grinding up on SAF demand can compress the spread from below. And the war sits in the residuals — no spread model predicts a strait closing or reopening. How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Marginals are triangular, with endpoints anchored to reported history (renewable margin low/high $370–$770 around your mid; volume 4.5–6.8 Mt; Oil Products EBITDA €340–1,040m; multiple 6–9×; M&S €105–150m). Renewable EBITDA = margin × volume ÷ 1.10 − (500 + 68 × volume); fair value = multiple × total EBITDA − net debt €3.6bn, ÷ 769.21m shares. Dependence: a one-factor copula (each driver loads on a common cycle factor — renewable margin 0.78, refining 0.70, multiple 0.45, volume 0.35, M&S 0.10, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob sets the stress probability). Correlation = 0 and tail = 0 reproduce the independent model exactly. Tornado, scenario anchors and the Bayesian posterior are all computed live. The loadings reflect relative cyclicality — a modelling choice, not estimated coefficients.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: a figure fetched from a citable source (Neste releases, S&P/Simply Wall St, Finnish Government, Yle, Zacks, Darling/MPC filings). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation outputs, the forensic scores, Neste's EV/EBITDA, the free-float figure). Your assumption: an input you set that is judgment, not data (mid-cycle margin, correlation, tail probability, scenario priors, evidence likelihood-ratios, kill thresholds).
