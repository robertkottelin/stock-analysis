Auto unit-economics — what has to be true

Decomposing the core leg into deliveries × ASP × margin.
Computed · model
MIXEDThe core is shrinking, and the base case needs it to stop. FY2025 deliveries fell to 1.64m (−8.6%) at a blended ASP near $42k; the pipeline’s $10bn 2028E core net income needs volume and margin to recover together — a reversal of the last two years, not an extrapolation of them.

The equation. Core automotive gross profit ≈ deliveries × ASP × gross margin. Everything the engine does to the core leg (net-income × P/E) is downstream of this line. So the entire auto slice of the sum-of-the-parts comes down to which cell in the grid below the world settles at — and the last two years have moved up and to the left (fewer units, thinner margin), not down and to the right.

Calibration. FY2025: automotive revenue $69.53bn on 1.64m deliveries ⇒ blended ASP ~$42k; auto GM ex-credits recovered to ~17.9% in Q4 (19.2% in Q1'26, but aided by ~$230m of warranty true-downs). Whether ASP holds as cheaper models mix in, and whether margin sticks without one-offs, decides the grid.

Robotaxi / autonomy optionality — the leg the price rests on

FSD → unsupervised robotaxi → a mobility network — priced as a risk-adjusted, discounted call option.
Computed · option leg
MIXEDReal, physically progressing, and the single biggest number in the valuation. But the leg is a risk-adjusted 2030E enterprise value (TAM × capture × probability) discounted five years. Why this is the leg. Tesla’s bull case has migrated almost entirely from selling cars to operating an autonomy network: a fleet of owned + third-party vehicles running unsupervised FSD, earning per-mile economics at software-like margins, plus FSD subscription revenue on the retail fleet. The gate is not manufacturing — it is unsupervised autonomy that regulators and physics accept at scale. As of mid-2026 that gate is partly open: unsupervised operation across a full metro, expansion into new cities, a Cybercab in volume production, and FSD moving subscription-only with record net-adds. None of it is yet a material, disclosed revenue line.

◆ Data providers & the audit rule

  • Yahoo Finance v8 chart API — public, non-authenticated JSON endpoint. Returns adjusted close, OHLC, volume and meta (regular-market-price, 52w range). Rate-limited but reliable for cash equities and major indices; used as the single source of truth for all live-fetched price series in Modules I & J.
  • Primary company releases — direct company disclosures (Finnish issuers publish concurrently in Finnish and English, ESMA-compliant). Every balance-sheet, income-statement and cash-flow figure inside Modules A / D / E traces to a release listed above.
  • Solidium / Finnish State ownership — the State's holding company and Prime Minister's Office publish the strategic-interest register directly.
  • Simply Wall St (S&P Global Market Intelligence) — used to cross-check balance-sheet ratios (coverage, gearing) where the primary release aggregates line items.
  • Analyst consensus — pulled from Investing.com and MarketScreener at time of write; consensus PTs are point-in-time and can move.

MetricFormula / definition
MC engine (Module A, re-used)Fair value per share = engine(driver draws) drawn from correlated triangulars with a one-factor copula and a regime-shock mixture. Exact formula per name is inside the pipeline's Module A footer.
Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.
Payoff-asymmetry ratio (Module I)(P(FV>price) × E[FV−price | FV>price]) ÷ (P(FV<price) × E[price−FV | FV<price]). Values > 1× = pay-off skew in your favour.
Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.
Kelly f* (Module I)(b·p − q)/b, where p = P(FV>price), q = 1−p. Fraction of bank-roll to size if the MC is truth. Half-Kelly (or lower) is the institutional convention.
VaR & CVaR (Module I)VaRα = the α-quantile of (FV/price − 1) — the loss threshold. CVaRα = mean of the α% worst paths — Expected Shortfall.
Realised volatility (Module I)SD of daily log-returns × √252, over trailing 1y (252 sessions) and 3y (756 sessions).
Beta (Module I)OLS slope of daily stock log-returns on benchmark log-returns over trailing 252 sessions. R² reported alongside.
Max drawdown (Module I)mint(Pt/max(Ps≤t) − 1) over 5-year adjusted closes.
Momentum 1/3/6/12m (Module I)Simple return over trailing 21 / 63 / 126 / 252 sessions on adjusted close.
Own-history percentile (Module I)Fraction of trailing 5-year daily closes ≤ current spot.
Peer 12m relative return (Module I)Stock's 12m return minus peer's 12m return, both simple returns on adjusted close.
Return base rates (Module J)Empirical frequencies over the 5-year rolling windows: P(63-day return < −10%), P(252-day return < 0), P(252-day return > +15%).

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths across four value legs. Marginals are triangular: core auto+services 2028E net income $5–18bn (mode $10bn; FY2025 group net income $3.79bn), core P/E 14–34× (mode 24×), energy 2028E revenue $20–58bn (mode $34bn; FY2025 $12.8bn, +26.6%), energy EV/Sales 3.5–11× (mode 6.5×), robotaxi 2030E risk-adjusted enterprise value $0–1,700bn (mode $450bn, your robotaxi slider), Optimus 2030E risk-adjusted enterprise value $0–1,500bn (mode $220bn, your Optimus slider). Fair value = [core NI × P/E + energy rev × EV/Sales] + [(robotaxi + Optimus) ÷ 1.10⁵] + net cash $30bn, × 1000 ÷ 3,510m shares. The two option legs are discounted a full five years (2030E) and their value is already risk-adjusted (TAM × capture × probability — see Modules M and N), so each is priced as a call option, not a base case. Dependence: a one-factor copula (loadings — robotaxi 0.90, Optimus 0.85, core NI 0.70, core P/E 0.65, energy revenue 0.55, energy multiple 0.50, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob). Correlation = 0 and tail = 0 reproduce the independent model exactly. The loadings reflect relative sentiment-sensitivity — a modelling choice, not estimated coefficients.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: a figure fetched from a citable source (the FY2025 / Q1'26 releases, SEC EDGAR, rollout coverage, the live Yahoo tape). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation outputs, the P/E multiples, the tornado, the Bayesian posterior). Your assumption: an input you set that is judgment, not data (the robotaxi and Optimus values, core net income, energy revenue, net-cash estimate, correlation, tail probability, scenario priors, likelihood ratios, kill thresholds).

Still excluded — post-Modules-I&J audit

Still legitimately excluded — this build kept these out, not faked: Beneish M-score — needs eight two-year ratios Tesla does not break out cleanly per segment; a half-estimated score is worse than none. Segment operating income for energy and services — Tesla discloses segment gross profit but not a clean segment net income, which is why the engine values a single core earnings block plus an energy EV/Sales leg rather than four fully-separated P&Ls — a modelling simplification, not a hidden number. A clean ex-one-off Q1'26 margin — the ~$230m warranty true-down and tariff refunds sit inside the reported 19.2% auto GM ex-credits; stripping them requires an allocation this build does not assert. Options-implied skew, daily short-interest and insider-transaction net flow — fetchable for a US mega-cap from paid terminals but not from the Yahoo v8 chart endpoint this toolkit standardises on. Everything else previously flagged as "cut" (factor betas vs S&P 500 / Nasdaq 100 / rates, price-history percentile, momentum, realised vol, drawdown, BYD/Toyota 12m relative, base-rate frequencies, owner-earnings yield vs real rate) is included in Modules I & J and computed live from the Yahoo Finance v8 chart API — and, unlike SpaceX, on a full multi-year sample.
