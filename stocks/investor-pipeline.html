Business thesis & macro place

What Investor is, where the value lives, and how the portfolio sits in AI · automation · energy · defence · health.
Sourced + judgment
BULL · THESISInvestor is Northern Europe’s best multi-decade industrial compounder — a three-leg portfolio (Listed 76% / Patricia 17% / EQT 7%) that has delivered 16.5% annualised total return over 20 years vs 10.2% for the SIXRX, at a 0.07% management cost and 1.9% leverage. The thesis is strong; the valuation debate is whether a stock trading at adjusted NAV still has edge, or whether the holdco discount that used to be free has already closed.

What the company is. Investor AB (Nasdaq Stockholm: INVE-A / INVE-B) is the Wallenberg family’s listed industrial holding company, founded in 1916. It is not an operating company and not a closed-end fund in the passive sense: it is an active owner of significant minority stakes in listed multinationals and of wholly-owned private platforms, with board influence, capital allocation rights and a multi-decade holding horizon. Adjusted net asset value on 30 Jun 2026 was SEK 1,214.7bn (SEK 397 per share); the B-share trades near that number.

◆ Place in the macro stack — five trend lenses

A holding company does not “do AI.” It owns the industrial layer that builds, automates, powers, defends and heals the world that AI is reshaping. The question is which waves the portfolio actually rides.

1 · AI & automation (core)
ABB · Atlas · Epiroc · Piab · Ericsson
~SEK 580bn+ of listed value in automation, robotics, industrial software, mining autonomy and AI-RAN. ABB alone is 23% of total assets. This is the structural growth spine of the portfolio — not a narrative overlay.
2 · Energy & electrification
ABB · Atlas · Wärtsilä · Epiroc
Grid, drives, compressors, marine/energy engines, battery-electric mining. Second-order AI (data-centre power, industrial efficiency) and first-order European energy transition. Durable multi-decade demand.
3 · Health & longevity
AstraZeneca · Sobi · Mölnlycke · Laborie · Nova
Listed biopharma (~SEK 150bn) plus Patricia’s health-tech platforms (~SEK 168bn of estimated MV). Defensive growth, high barriers, cash generative. The shock absorber when industrials cycle.
4 · Defence & security
Saab · (Ericsson dual-use)
Saab (~SEK 83bn, 30% owned) is a direct NATO / European rearmament call option with a multi-year backlog. Geopolitical regime change is already in the numbers; the question is duration.
5 · Data & capital markets
Nasdaq · SEB · EQT
Market infrastructure, Nordic banking and private-markets compounding. Less glamorous than robotics, but high-ROE cash engines that fund the dividend and the dry powder.
Investment thesis (one paragraph). Own Investor if you want a diversified, actively-owned slice of European industrial and health excellence — automation, electrification, defence, rare-disease and wound-care platforms — run at almost no cost (7 bp), with fortress leverage (1.9%), a rising dividend and a 20-year record of beating the market by ~6 pp a year. Fade it if you believe at-NAV is full for a holdco (the historical free lunch was the 10–20% discount, and it is gone), or if a broad equity bear market will crush the 76% listed book and re-open a wide discount simultaneously. The whole report is built around that fault line: is quality-at-NAV still a buy, or does the closed discount mean the edge has been priced away?

◆ Every metric computed here — formulas

MetricFormula / definition
MC engine (Module A, re-used)Fair value per share = engine(driver draws) drawn from correlated triangulars with a one-factor copula and a regime-shock mixture. Exact formula per name is inside the pipeline's Module A footer.
Reverse-DCF (Module I)Solve engine(driver=x, all others at mode) = spot for x, by 80-step grid search over the driver's pipeline range. Reports x, the implied value at mode, and the % gap vs pipeline mode.
Forward P/E (Module I)Live spot ÷ the sourced consensus EPS estimate for the current fiscal year (≈ next 12 months) and the next fiscal year (≈ 24 months). Trailing P/E uses the reported TTM EPS. A non-positive EPS (loss year) renders as n/m.
Payoff-asymmetry ratio (Module I)(P(FV>price) × E[FV−price | FV>price]) ÷ (P(FV<price) × E[price−FV | FV<price]). Values > 1× = pay-off skew in your favour.
Win/loss ratio b (Module I)b = E[gain | FV>price] ÷ E[loss | FV<price], both in the stock's price currency.
Kelly f* (Module I)(b·p − q)/b, where p = P(FV>price), q = 1−p. Fraction of bank-roll to size if the MC is truth. Half-Kelly (or lower) is the institutional convention.
VaR & CVaR (Module I)VaRα = the α-quantile of (FV/price − 1) — the loss threshold. CVaRα = mean of the α% worst paths — Expected Shortfall.
Realised volatility (Module I)SD of daily log-returns × √252, over trailing 1y (252 sessions) and 3y (756 sessions).
Beta (Module I)OLS slope of daily stock log-returns on benchmark log-returns over trailing 252 sessions. R² reported alongside.
Max drawdown (Module I)mint(Pt/max(Ps≤t) − 1) over 5-year adjusted closes.
Momentum 1/3/6/12m (Module I)Simple return over trailing 21 / 63 / 126 / 252 sessions on adjusted close.
Own-history percentile (Module I)Fraction of trailing 5-year daily closes ≤ current spot.
Peer 12m relative return (Module I)Stock's 12m return minus peer's 12m return, both simple returns on adjusted close.
Return base rates (Module J)Empirical frequencies over the 5-year rolling windows: P(63-day return < −10%), P(252-day return < 0), P(252-day return > +15%).
Factor composite V/Q/M/L (Module J)V = 1 − price-percentile in own 5y history; Q = quality anchor from Module D (Piotroski / Altman / ROCE); M = logistic-scaled (12m − 1m) return; L = 1 − (1y realised vol / 45%). Simple mean × 100.
Owner-earnings yield − real rate (Module J)OE yield = pipeline Module D FCF / market cap; real rate = ^TNX (fetched at build) minus a 2.0% implied inflation. Spread in bp.

How the engine computes (Module A)

20,000 paths. Each driver is drawn from a triangular distribution: listed SEK bn (720–1,180, mode 946), Patricia SEK bn (160–270, mode 208), EQT SEK bn (55–125, mode 88), net debt SEK bn (10–55, mode 23.3), holdco discount % (−5 to +20, mode 5). Fair value = (listed + patricia + eqt − nd) × 1,000 / 3,062.9m shares × (1 − disc/100). Dependence: a one-factor copula (loadings — listed 0.80, eqt 0.55, patricia 0.45, disc 0.35, nd 0.20, scaled by your correlation knob) plus a regime-shock mixture (your tail knob). Correlation 0 and tail 0 reproduce the independent model. Tornado, scenario anchors and the Bayesian posterior are computed live and mirror stocks/config_investor.py.

The three labels, precisely

Sourced: fetched from a citable source (Investor interim/annual reports, the Yahoo fundamentals feed, sell-side consensus). Computed: calculated here from sourced inputs (the simulation, P/NAV, portfolio sums). Your assumption: an input you set (holdco discount mode, listed stress, correlation, tail, scenario priors, evidence weights, kill thresholds).

Still excluded — post-Modules-I&J audit

Still legitimately excluded — this build kept these out, not faked: IFRS consolidated earnings / trailing P/E — dominated by unrealised fair-value changes in listed stakes; a 4.6× trailing P/E is an accounting artefact, not a value signal. The engine therefore values Investor on adjusted NAV × (1 − holdco discount), which is how the company and the market actually think about it. Full private-company DCF for every Patricia subsidiary — Investor discloses estimated market values and applied EV/EBITDA multiples for the majors; re-underwriting Mölnlycke from scratch would add noise, not signal. We use Investor's own estimated MVs as the Patricia leg and stress them in the MC. Beneish M-score / manufacturing forensics — meaningless on a holding-company balance sheet. Options-implied skew, precise borrow cost, daily insider-flow — not reliably fetchable from a public API. Everything else previously "cut" (factor betas, own-history percentile, momentum, realised vol, drawdown, peer 12m relative, base-rate frequencies, owner-earnings yield vs real rate) is included in Modules I & J and computed live from the Yahoo Finance v8 chart API.
